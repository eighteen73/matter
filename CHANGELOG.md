# Changelog

## 1.0.0

A rework that moves design decisions out of the editor and into the theme. Not compatible with 0.x content; keep existing sites on 0.x. The README's theme contract lists what themes and saved content can rely on within 1.x.

### Added

**Authoring**

- **Sections:** layout blocks declared in `block.json` (`supports.matter.slots`), with locked `matter/slot` content areas, a shared editor, a default renderer and `Matter\slots()` for custom `render.php`.
  - Slots are matched by name, so they can be added, reordered or removed in `block.json`; a renamed slot lists its old names (`previously`). Content in a slot that's no longer declared isn't output, and the editor keeps it, marked, for the client to move or remove. A slot's `alignments` are offered to the blocks in it (wide and full, say, for a carousel wider than the text around it).
  - An empty slot is marked `is-empty` (on the front end, one that renders nothing; in the editor, one with no blocks). A section's variants are block styles (`styles` in `block.json`); one in its default variant gets that variant's class (`is-style-default`), on the front end and in the editor.
  - Lock a slot with `templateLock: "all"` or `"insert"`, not `"contentOnly"`, which hides the blocks' settings behind "Edit pattern". A locked slot applies a changed template only when it keeps every saved block, at any depth: a block the template adds is added, and one that replaces a saved block never overwrites it.
  - The list view names a section after its first heading (plain text, shortened; a name the client gives it wins) and a slot in sentence case (`media-end` is "Media end"), with its own icon. Sections, modals, drawers and collapsibles are previewed in the inserter from their slots' templates, unless they declare an `example`; Matter's other blocks have sample content, and posts, products and terms displays preview three items.
  - Slots are found with a marker scan, not a regular expression, which failed past about 1 MB of slot HTML and left every slot empty. Nested slots of the same name pair correctly, and a slot name with characters outside letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores is reported when its block registers.
- **Whole-block links:** `supports.matter.link`, saved in `matterLink` (`{ url, opensInNewTab }`), e.g. for cards, replacing pulsar-extensions' group link. The first heading becomes the link, stretched over the block (`has-link`, `.matter-link`). Without a heading, a visually hidden link is named after the block's first paragraph (shortened) or first image description, else "Read more" (`matter_link_label`). A link that opens in a new tab says so, in the hidden link's name or in a `.matter-link__cue` that describes the heading's link and that a theme's rules for spans can't show. An unsafe address (`javascript:`) links nothing, and `<abbr>` in a heading no longer loses the heading link.
- **Colour schemes:** `supports.matter.scheme`, a swatch control (each named under its swatch), and CSS generated from the theme's `custom.scheme`, with variable names matching core's exactly and values that can't comment out, fetch or swallow later rules. `matter_schemes` is always applied; a scheme it adds, or a role it changes, is written out as its own value (it has no theme.json variable); a scheme's `label` names it in the editor and isn't emitted as a variable.
- **Variants:** theme-defined block presets via `matter_variants`, shown as block styles.
- **Breakpoints:** theme-owned (`matter_breakpoints`), in px, em or rem. An em or rem breakpoint stays em or rem in the media queries, so it follows the visitor's text size; a map that mixes px with em or rem is reported once, any other unit is refused with a notice, and a responsive map with no base entry starts at its first entry's value.
- **Menus:** Appearance › Menus rendered by `Matter\menu()`, in dropdown, accordion and drill-down types that can switch per breakpoint without JavaScript, with megamenus built from the menu's structure and optional theme panels (`template-parts/megamenu/`, from the parent and child theme, the child's winning).
- **Panels:** `Matter\panel()` and `panel_toggle()`, off-canvas below a breakpoint and inline above it. A panel used twice gets `-2`, and the panel script pairs each toggle with the nearest panel of its name (`data-matter-panel`), whatever order they render in; `Panel::render()` and `Trigger::button()` take `process` (false in a block's render), and the button takes `content`.
- **Archives:** content above and below post type, taxonomy, author, date, search and 404 lists, edited like pages (Appearance › Archives). `matter/archive-content` wraps a template's list and `matter/archive-loop` places it, with variants and a live preview. An entry is for one archive (`_matter_archive_target`); terms use their taxonomy's entry, or a parent's or the post type's when that entry is set to cascade (off by default); the newest published entry wins, so new versions can be scheduled.
  - `author` and `date` targets (one entry for all author archives, one for all date archives) no longer count as the blog: each looks for its own entry, then the blog's only if it cascades. Their title binding is `get_the_archive_title()`.
  - The `matter/archive` bindings source gives the archive's title, description, count and image; a bound block's sidebar says where its text is edited. Page 2 and later show the archive's title as the one H1 and the list, without the entry's intro (`matter_archive_paged_content`). Customising an archive starts an unsaved draft.
  - Preview shows the exact draft: an entry that isn't published has `preview_id=<ID>` on its link, and the fallback to the newest editable draft is gone, so two drafts for one archive each preview as themselves.
  - Entries can't be listed: `/?post_type=matter_archive`, its feed and `?p=<ID>` of an unpublished entry are 404s, entries are taken out of the front end's main query (so no feed or theme loop lists them) and out of the core sitemap, and the REST API serves them only to users who can edit them. Only public taxonomies can have an entry, and a password-protected entry never shows on its archive (the default content shows, not an older entry).
  - Registered post type and taxonomy names are lowercased mid-sentence in English locales only (`matter_archive_lowercase_names`).
- **Site settings:** config-driven fields, a React settings page (Appearance › Site details), `Matter\setting()` and the `matter/setting` bindings source. A `url` field adds `https://` to an address typed without it and keeps a relative one (`/…`, `#…`, `?…`, `./…`, `../…`, or a word or path with no dot), which can't become another site's address; a number must be finite; a save merges over stored values, so a field not sent keeps its value. A `url` or `href` binding is cleaned once, so `&` isn't doubled.
- **Loop items:** `matter/loop-item` shows the theme's pattern for an item (`{namespace}/loop-item-{post_type}`, else `{namespace}/loop-item`; `matter_loop_item_pattern`) in every loop and carousel, previewed in the editor as on the front end, and supports client navigation. Carousels, accordions and tabs of posts can have their own (`loop-item-{post_type}-accordion`, `loop-item-accordion`…), in a template-hierarchy order, and a variant its own (`loop-item-carousel-{variant}`); the filters are given the variant. The block renders nothing outside a loop.
- **Term loop items and terms carousels:** in a Terms Query's term template, `matter/loop-item` shows the theme's pattern for the term (`{namespace}/loop-item-term-{taxonomy}`, else `{namespace}/loop-item-term`; `matter_loop_item_term_pattern`), with the term's `termId` and `taxonomy` context, else a link to the term. A Terms Query can be shown as a carousel (`matterDisplay`), as "Terms carousel" in the inserter and the carousel's picker.
- **Query lists:** every loop template's list has the class `matter-query-list`, in the front end and the editor; carousels and accordions of a query find their items by it. Empty accordion items and tabs (content that renders nothing to show) aren't output, and a skipped tab leaves the tab list.
- **Editor:** Matter's blocks use core's icons (`@wordpress/icons`) rather than Dashicons. A `core/button` with text but no link is outlined and tagged "No link", as are archive content and the archive list in an entry (editor only). A filter block can sit among other blocks (a heading, a note between filters).
- **Core block extensions:** button icon (`matterIcon` on `core/button`, chosen from a core-style icon library; `matter_icon_collections`), focal point (core's own `focalPoint` on every image block, and on featured images, which need an image), and `sizes` for wide and full-width images so they aren't downloaded at the chosen size and stretched.
- **Theme files and names:** `matter/template-part` renders a PHP file from the theme's `template-parts/` in block templates, with its editor preview including the styles the part enqueues; only a listed part renders (files with a `Title:` header and `matter_template_parts`, never `megamenu/*`). `matter_allowed_blocks` (with `namespace/*`) is enforced in the post editor, and a block a section's slot allows is offered only in those sections. The theme's `assets/svg/icons/` SVGs are an icon collection (`matter_icon_theme_collection`), registered on `init` so core's Icon block and `wp_get_icon()` find them. `matter_theme_namespaces` (and `Matter\theme_namespaces()`) names the prefix of the theme's loop item patterns and icon collection, so they don't depend on the folder name.
- **Theme contract:** the README lists what themes and saved content depend on (names, markup, stored data and their formats), which changes only in a major version, and what a minor release may change. Snapshot tests cover the markup it names.
- **Integrations:** what Matter does with WooCommerce and Gravity Forms lives in integrations (`Integrations\WooCommerce`, `Integrations\GravityForms`), booted on `plugins_loaded` only when the plugin is active; Matter's own code names no other plugin. They add through filters a project can use too (see Hooks below) and `wp.hooks` filters in the editor (`matter.queryDisplay.carouselTemplate`, `matter.queryDisplay.displayAttributes`, `matter.carousel.queryOptions`). WooCommerce's editor code (the Products carousel) is its own script, loaded only with WooCommerce.

**Filters and live content**

- **Filters:** `matter/filters`, `matter/filter`, `matter/filters-toggle`, `matter/active-filters` and `matter/results-count`, replacing FacetWP. Filters go inside a Query Loop (or a Product Collection, with WooCommerce): a sidebar that's a drawer below a breakpoint, or a bar of dropdowns; options with counts, chips, swatches, stars (with "& up"), range sliders, collapsible options and a results count. The URL is the state (`filter_{name}`, with `filter_{name}_match=all` and `_min`/`_max`; WooCommerce's sources translate theirs into its own parameters), so it works without JavaScript; with it, the `matter/filters` store navigates with the Interactivity router, or a registered results provider. Sources: any public taxonomy, and with WooCommerce product categories, tags, brands, attributes (with their swatches), price, stock and rating. A filtered listing is `noindex, follow` (`matter_filters_noindex`). A filterable query block inside another block navigates without a page load.
- **Filters in the editor:** their own "Filters" category; a filter is shown in the front end's markup with its name edited in place, a collapsible one open or closed as set, and a bar's options below their button. Settings are a tools panel, offering the sources for what the listing lists; variations are named as their source is, with its icon.
- **For results providers:** `matter_filters_listing`, `matter_filters_base_query_vars` and `matter_filters_page_param`, `resultsState()` in `matter/filters`, range chips worded in the browser (`range_labels()`), and `Live::expect()`.
- **Live blocks:** blocks a plugin declares live (`matter_live_blocks`: a price that depends on the customer, a stock level) become keyed placeholders in loop items, filled in the browser from `GET matter/v1/live` (script module `matter/live`). Guests' answers are cacheable until the next scheduled change (`matter_live_expires`, at most five minutes), versioned by `matter_live_version`; logged-in visitors' are private, and one without a REST nonce is given one. Placeholders are made for search index cards, and for pages if a site turns it on (`matter_live_placeholders`).
- **Prerendered loop items:** `LoopItems\Prerendered::render()` returns an item's loop item rendered ahead of time, as a guest, so it can be stored (a search index), with a version that says when it's stale (`matter_prerendered_loop_item`, `matter_prerendered_loop_item_version`, `matter_prerender_loop_item_start` and `_end`). With WooCommerce, products are rendered without the shopper's cart.
- **Prices of their own:** with WooCommerce, when a pricing plugin says the shopper has them (`matter_own_prices`), price filters and sorting use their prices (`matter_product_prices`), and a results provider leaves those clicks to the server.

**Scripting**

- **JavaScript API:** `matter.carousel()`, `matter.tabs()`, `matter.accordion()`, `matter.gallery()`, `matter.overlay()`, `matter.panel()` and `matter.filters()` return instances with methods and read-only state; `matter.ready()` waits for a block (it accepts `#id`, and resolves with `null` once the page has loaded and the target isn't in or around a block); every block dispatches `matter-{block}-*` events, all through one helper, so every event has `detail.id` and `detail.instance`. `matter-overlay-open` fires once the dialog is shown, including an open from the URL hash on load. The block stores take a target (ID or element) too. A small stub in the page's head defines `window.matter` before any module loads, and Matter's own state sits under `Symbol.for( 'matter' )`, so nothing on `window.matter` starts with an underscore. The carousel's `embla` and `reInit( options )` are documented as unstable.
- **Shared script modules:** `matter/focus-trap` (one trap stack for menus and panels; a trap told to rest before it is active waits to be resumed) and `matter/inert` (what panels, drill-down menus and overlays hold inert, reference-counted, so a modal opened from an open panel tells the panel's hold to let go) are loaded once.
- **Overlay triggers:** one delegated click listener opens overlays, so triggers added after load work; a link opens one only when it goes to the same page, and a modified, non-primary, new-tab or download click is left to the browser. A dialog or panel removed while open releases the scroll lock and focus trap.

**IDs and links**

- **Stable hashes:** tabs, accordions and posts displays get an ID of their own when added (`matterId`; an HTML anchor replaces it), and items in posts tabs and accordions are named after their post (`#{id}-post-{postId}`). A link to an item, or anything in it, opens it and anything it's nested in, also on `#hash` changes; an invalid hash no longer throws.
- **Unique IDs:** every ID Matter outputs from saved data (a tabs or accordion block's and its items', an overlay's, a menu's and its items', a panel's, a carousel's or gallery's anchor) is claimed per page (`Ids::claim()`): a block rendered twice gets `-2`, `-3`. A saved overlay ID is cleaned as the editor cleans it, and an overlay without a saved ID doesn't remember dismissal (`localStorage` key `matter_overlay_dismissed_{id}`).

**Without JavaScript**

- **The page and block marks:** a script in the page's head adds `matter-js` to `<html>` and, four seconds after the page is parsed, `matter-late`; each block's script adds `is-enhanced` to its own element (an accordion's items, tabs' panels, a panel's toggles too) and `matter-ready-{type}` to `<html>`. The CSS that hides content applies to a block that is `is-enhanced`, or is about to be (`matter-js`, and either not yet `matter-late` or its type's script has run), so content stays visible when JavaScript is off or a block's script fails, a block inserted later by client-side navigation doesn't flash, and a type whose script never starts shows its content after four seconds whatever the others do.
- **The enhanced gate:** `enhanced($type)` (`src/styles/_enhanced.scss`) is `:is(.is-enhanced, html.matter-js:is(:not(.matter-late), .matter-ready-{type}) *, .editor-styles-wrapper *)`. A theme that copies it (Pulsar's `matter-enhanced()`) changes with it; a Jest test fails if Matter's gate changes without this line. It reads Matter's files only, so a theme keeps its own copy in step (Pulsar's `npm run lint:gate` compares its copy with this line's string).
- **Per block:** the server renders no `inert` or `hidden`, and the script adds them.
  - Accordion, collapsible and tabs show every answer and panel; tabs show each panel under its name (`.wp-block-matter-tab__label`, a heading at level 3, `matter_tab_label_level`) and no tab list or dropdown, and panels aren't `role="tabpanel"` until the script adds that (`state.panelRole`, `panelTabIndex`, `panelLabelledBy`). An accordion item's button renders `aria-expanded` only when it is open by default.
  - A closed panel is `hidden="until-found"` once the script has started, so find-in-page, a text fragment or a link into it opens the item or tab (`beforematch`; an accordion item opens without its animation, a collapsible's closed content and an inactive panel keep no margin, padding or border); elsewhere it is plainly hidden.
  - A modal or drawer's first trigger link is a real link to the dialog (`href="#{id}"`), which shows it in the page, as content, only where no script is coming.
  - Menus show every link, with dropdowns opening on `:hover` and `:focus-within` (a label-only parent shows its toggle so the keyboard can reach its submenu), accordion and drill-down menus as nested open lists, and a megamenu within its nearest positioned ancestor. A panel is inline at every width, with no toggle.
**Accessibility**

- **Panels and drill-down:** an open off-canvas panel makes the rest of the page `inert` (its own close button, inside the dialog, and any live region or modal excepted), so only the dialog is exposed; Matter's `inert` comes off exactly where it went on, and a failure while opening or closing can't leave the page inert. A drill-down submenu makes the level it covers `inert`, restored by Back, Escape, closing the panel or a change of menu type; the panel's close button stays in the Tab cycle. Escape closes the topmost panel, a submenu open in it first, and a modal opened from it before the panel. A panel's `open()` and `toggle()` do nothing while it is inline.
- **Focus and keys:** a modal, drawer, panel or lightbox gives focus back to the control that opened it, even in Safari, where a click doesn't focus a button; a change of breakpoint that closes the panel leaves focus where it is. Tabs and menus swap Left and Right for a right-to-left element (read from the element), and a dropdown flips on the edge it overflows. Tabs and accordions leave keys pressed with Alt, Ctrl or Meta to the browser; the gallery lightbox's Home and End go to the first and last image.
- **Names and landmarks:** a modal or drawer without a label is named by its own first heading (`aria-labelledby`, set on the server), else "Dialog"; accordion panels are `role="region"` only in an accordion of six or fewer items (a Query Loop accordion counts the query's total); accordion titles and tab labels allow inline formatting only; carousel slides get `role="group"` only on elements that allow it, and carousel and gallery regions get unique names ("Label (2)"). The lightbox has a polite live region saying "Image N of M" and the image's description, announced on open and cleared on close. A saved icon the theme no longer has shows as missing in the editor instead of a 404.
- **Scroll lock:** an open modal, drawer, panel or lightbox keeps the scrollbar's space (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`, only on a page that scrolls, measured from the scrollbar's thickness), so the page and a fixed header don't jump sideways. One reference-counted lock (`has-scroll-gutter`) is shared, so a panel and a modal open together in either order keep the page steady; `has-overlay-open` and `has-panel-open` are unchanged.
- **Gallery lightbox:** a modal like any other, so over an open panel it takes the same suspension of the held page and the shared scroll lock.

**Hooks**

- **One stem per area:** the README's Hooks section lists every hook outside Filters, Live blocks and Prerendered loop items with its arguments; any other hook is internal.
- **New:** `matter_register_block` (`$register`, `$name`, `$folder`: whether a block registers, answered by one callback), `matter_theme_namespaces`, `matter_icon_theme_collection`, `matter_archive_paged_content`, `matter_archive_lowercase_names` and `matter_tab_label_level`; for integrations, `matter_integrations`, `matter_query_blocks`, `matter_query_loops`, `matter_query_display_post_types`, `matter_archive_post_type_title`, `matter_archive_term_image_id` and `matter_hidden_blocks`; for Filters, `matter_filter_sources`, `matter_filterable_query_blocks`, `matter_pre_filter_counts`, `matter_filter_swatch` and `matter_filter_param_name`.

**Tooling**

- **Requirements:** WordPress 7.1 (from 7.1 beta 1, so an alpha build is refused) and PHP 8.3, checked before anything loads: an older one gets an admin notice, not a fatal error. WooCommerce 11 for its integration; an older one switches it off, with a notice for users who can activate plugins.
- **Tests:** PHPUnit with contract markup snapshots (menus, panel, gallery, scheme CSS, links, slots and more), PHPUnit for megamenu saves, the walker, triggers, both bindings sources and every REST route's permissions, JavaScript unit tests and a Playwright and axe browser suite (Chromium, and Firefox and WebKit for dialogs, focus, panels and menus), run in CI against wp-env, also on a site without WooCommerce or Gravity Forms, and weekly against WordPress nightly and the latest WooCommerce.
- **Translation:** `languages/matter.pot` (`npm run make-pot`) and the languages folder passed to `wp_set_script_translations()`. Sentences are one translatable string each, translated nouns aren't lowercased, and labels with counts read `%d`, `%s` and numbered placeholders.
- **Release:** the shared release and lint workflows are referenced by commit, so a change to their repository can't change Matter's release.

### Changed

- **Accordion:** two blocks (accordion, item), with FAQ schema output inline. An accordion of posts is a Query Loop shown as one ("Posts accordion"), its content the theme's `loop-item-accordion` pattern. Posts accordions and tabs list up to 100 items (core's most) rather than 10, since nothing pages through them, and the inserter finds posts displays by the site's post type names ("faq" finds Posts accordion).
- **Tabs:** two blocks (tabs, tab). Tabs of posts are a Query Loop shown as tabs ("Posts tabs"). The tab list is built on the server. Horizontal or vertical, optional tab icons (chosen on the block toolbar), tabs removed with Backspace in an empty name or "Delete tab", and optionally a dropdown on smaller screens (showing tab icons), where the theme sets the breakpoint.
- **Carousel:** one block. Slides are its inner blocks, and behaviour comes from variants. A carousel of posts or products is the Query Loop or Product Collection itself (`matterDisplay`), shown as "Posts carousel" and "Products carousel" with the query's own filters. The theme's slide types (`matter_carousel_slides`) are offered when a carousel is added; one with `within` only in carousels inside those blocks; a carousel that already has a variant keeps it.
- **Gallery:** grid or carousel layout, with a native `<dialog>` lightbox. Editors choose the columns (from the theme's choices) and an aspect ratio preset; the theme sets the image size. The grid can show the first N images, in whole rows, with a "View all" button. Images are managed in the media library's gallery mode, as in core's Gallery. Captions show behind an info button, over the image or not at all, and in the lightbox. Lightbox captions and FAQ schema text are decoded (`&amp;` showed as typed), an image outside the media library opens as itself, and Embla is bundled once.
- **Modal, drawer and collapsible:** single blocks with an optional trigger area and a content area, also opened by any link to `#id`. Modal and drawer use native `<dialog>` and can be made non-closable (e.g. an age gate); modals in a Query Loop get one instance per post, and grouped modals have previous/next, keeping the backdrop while switching. URL parameters open an overlay even after a dismissal. In the editor the canvas shows only what the front end shows, an overlay opening as it does on the front end while it's selected; an Overlays sidebar lists and adds modals and drawers, link search suggests overlays by name, and buttons that open one get "Edit modal" (or drawer, collapsible). Modals, drawers and gallery lightboxes animate out in every browser (`is-closing`).
- **Gravity Form block:** it loses its `align`, colour (background, gradients), spacing (padding, margin) and border supports, as layout and styling are the theme's; it registers only with the Gravity Forms integration (`matter_register_block`).
- **CSS:** functional rules at one class (context in `:where()`) plus a minimal default skin entirely in `:where()`, motion and icons included, so one theme class overrides any of it wherever the theme's stylesheet loads. Selectors are written out in full, with nesting only for the same element, enforced by stylelint; `:is()` is left as written (a panel's inline CSS is about 4 KB, not 12). No custom-property fallback chains and no inline styles from attributes. Block variables are named `--{block}-{setting}` (e.g. `--gallery-gap`, `--carousel-per-view`); colour schemes set 11 roles (`background`, `background-surface`, `background-fill`, `text`, `text-subtle`, `text-heading`, `text-link`, `text-fill`, `border`, `border-strong`, `highlight`) as `--scheme--{role}`. Modals and drawers fit the viewport inside a constrained layout; a modal's cap is `--modal-max-inline-size`, which a full-screen variant sets to `100vw`.
- **Direction:** one stylesheet per component, with logical properties and `:dir(rtl)`, instead of flipped `-rtl.css` copies. Drawer and panel sides are `start` and `end` (drawer `position`, `.is-position-start`; panel `position`, `.is-from-start`), not left and right.
- **Carousel settings:** `--carousel-per-view` and `--carousel-gap` set slide widths and gaps.
- **Core blocks Matter replaces:** hidden with `supports.inserter` instead of rewriting the allowed-blocks list.
- **Dependencies:** locked (`package-lock.json`, `composer.lock`); unused packages removed.
- **Renames since the first 1.0 work**, all free before a release:
  - Attributes and markup: the whole-block link is saved in `matterLink`, not `link` (`supports.matter.link` is unchanged); the gallery's `data-wp-init--api` and `--rows` are `data-wp-init---api` and `---rows`, the directive-ID syntax of WordPress 7.1; menu items' IDs are `{menu ID}-item-{ID}` instead of core's `menu-item-{ID}` (the class is unchanged).
  - Scripting: the overlay's `previous()` is `prev()`, as on the other blocks (the store action `actions.prev` and the group button's directive too), and an overlay instance has `closeAll()`; the accordion item no longer receives the unused `matter/autoclose` and `matter/openFirst` context from a query.
  - Hooks: `matter_theme_namespace` is `matter_theme_namespaces`; `matter_theme_icons` is `matter_icon_theme_collection`; `matter_register_{slug}` is `matter_register_block`, with the block's name (`matter/carousel`) as an argument; the 0.x drill-down texts became `matter_menu_back_text` and `matter_menu_view_all_text`.
  - Behaviour: schemes and variants that no longer exist: a scheme slug that isn't defined renders no scheme class (in the editor too, where it used to add one the front end ignored), so the block inherits its parent's; a section whose saved `is-style-*` names a variant that is no longer registered gets the default variant's class, in both; a theme that styled a closed panel through `[inert]` (or the collapsible's `[hidden]`, or an inactive tab's) should use `:not(.is-open)`, `:not(.is-active)`, or the same selectors under `.is-enhanced`.
- **Fixes that change output:** a `matter/loop-item` outside a loop renders nothing (a saved Products carousel with WooCommerce inactive showed the page as a card); `Archives::allow_archive_loop()` leaves an allow-all list alone (it used to drop every block registered only in JavaScript from the inserter); Matter no longer calls core's private `_wp_to_kebab_case()`.

### Removed

- **0.x hooks:** `matter_navigation_drill_down_back_text` and `matter_navigation_drill_down_view_all_text` (now `matter_menu_back_text` and `matter_menu_view_all_text`), `matter_navigation_render_inner_block` and `matter_query_loop_template_blocks`.
- **Navigation:** `matter/navigation` and `matter/navigation-megamenu` blocks, and the megamenu template-part area (replaced by menus).
- **Carousel children:** `carousel-viewport`, `carousel-slide`, `carousel-thumbnails`, `carousel-dots`, `carousel-progress`, `carousel-previous-button`, `carousel-next-button`.
- **Accordion and tab children:** `accordion-heading`, `accordion-panel`, `tab-list`, `tab-button`, `tab-panels`, `tab-panel`.
- **Overlay pieces:** `modal-content`, `drawer-content`, `collapsible-content`, `trigger`, `trigger-hamburger`, `close`, `modal-navigation` (now built into the modal's group option).
- **Per-instance design controls:** colours, spacing, borders, breakpoints, backdrop and size settings.
- **Legacy shared code:** `config/block-styles.json` and the block styles engine, the old overlay and lightbox stores, and the ID/context helpers.

### Visual changes

Changes to the default skin (in `:where()`). From 1.0 the skin changes only in a major version, or to fix a bug or an accessibility failure; see the theme contract in the README.

- The skin is minimal: no radii (modal, dropdown, tooltip, caption and carousel buttons), shadows, font weights (toggles, tabs, labels), decorative borders (accordion items, the tab list, drill-down rows and header, carousel arrows) or opacity effects (thumbnails, menu descriptions).
- Dropdown submenus and the tabs dropdown have a border instead of a shadow; the selected tab is underlined (both orientations) instead of a border on its side.
- Surfaces fall back to the system colours `Canvas` and `CanvasText` without a scheme, and tints (progress and timer tracks, borders) are mixed from the text colour, so they show on dark schemes.
- In forced colours, a modal, drawer, off-canvas panel and gallery lightbox have a border and an outline (the system text colour), as their background and backdrop aren't shown; the lightbox also gets an opaque background and matching text so its controls stay visible. The outline is there for a theme that resets `border` on its dialogs (which outranks the `:where()` border); it takes no space, and on a drawer or panel only its page-facing edge is on the screen. Icons, dots, progress bars and the panel toggle's bars stay visible.
- While a modal, drawer, panel or lightbox is open on a page that scrolls, the page keeps its scrollbar's space (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), so content and fixed headers don't shift sideways, and the backdrop stops at the reserved strip. On a page too short to scroll, nothing is reserved. In Chromium, `100vw` is then the viewport without the gutter, so a width worked out in `vw` is up to a pixel smaller while it is open.
- Carousel dots are sized in `rem` (0.625rem) and spaced with their centres 24px apart; menu toggles, back buttons and links, and overlay close buttons, are at least 24px, for WCAG 2.5.8's target size. Reduced motion also stops the transition between grouped modals.
- A closed collapsible's content and an inactive tab's panel (`hidden="until-found"`) have no margin, padding, border or minimum height of their own, and an accordion panel also transitions `content-visibility`, so an `until-found` panel stays visible while it collapses.
- Without JavaScript (or when a block's script fails):
  - a modal or drawer that is linked to shows in the page: a block with the skin's background, no backdrop, no motion and no close button;
  - a drill-down or accordion menu is a nested list (submenus indented, open), a dropdown opens on hover and focus, a megamenu stays within its nearest positioned ancestor, and a panel is an inline list with no toggle, instead of a hidden drawer;
  - accordion headings have no caret (and the ordinary cursor) and every answer shows; tabs show every panel under its name, in bold (`.wp-block-matter-tab__label`, hidden once the script starts), with no tab list; a collapsible's content shows.
